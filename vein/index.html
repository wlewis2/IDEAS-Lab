Vein Camera

Live vein visualization: the venous spectral index R − x·G, enhanced with contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalization. CLAHE tiles are set up automatically from the scene — sized from the detected vein width and elongated along the vein — or manually under “Adjust”. Global equalization and the raw index are available for comparison.
